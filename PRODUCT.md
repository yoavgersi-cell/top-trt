# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: US men who suspect or have confirmed low testosterone and are
researching online TRT (testosterone replacement therapy) clinics in order to
compare options, choose a provider, and start treatment via telehealth. They
arrive mostly from search, often comparing a few providers and weighing
cost, legitimacy, and fit.

## Product Purpose

toptrt.io ("Top TRT") is an independent comparison publisher that ranks and
reviews online TRT clinics and publishes deep educational content about TRT.
It exists to help a man choose the right clinic for his situation. Success =
the visitor clicks through to a recommended clinic (the primary conversion is
an affiliate click-out to a ranked provider).

## Positioning

An independent, honest, no-pay-to-rank comparison backed by a transparent
ranking methodology and medical review (E-E-A-T) - a trustworthy third-party
evaluator, not a clinic selling its own service. Rankings reflect real
assessment and are never for sale.

## Operating Context

Visitors come largely from organic search and AI engines (content is built for
SEO/GEO/AEO). They compare providers on a ranked homepage, read provider
reviews, head-to-head comparisons, per-state availability pages, "best TRT
for X" landing pages, and long-form guides; some use a Find-Your-Match quiz.
They then click out to a provider. Monetization is affiliate (RevOffers
tracking links).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Content-driven site: ranked provider comparison, provider reviews,
  head-to-head comparison pages, programmatic per-state pages, segment landing
  pages, deep article pillars, and a provider-matching quiz.
- SEO/GEO/AEO structure: answer-first content, question-headed FAQ sections,
  Article/Breadcrumb/Person/FAQPage schema.
- YMYL health constraints (binding): accuracy above all; no fabricated
  statistics, prices, studies, or reviews; TRT framed as prescription-only and
  clinician-directed; testosterone is a controlled substance.
- Commercial/technical constraints: FTC affiliate disclosure must stay visible;
  affiliate links carry rel="sponsored nofollow"; canonical host is
  www.toptrt.io; review figures shown as on-page feedback, not self-serving
  star-rating structured data.

## Brand Commitments

- Name/identity: "Top TRT" (toptrt.io).
- BINDING: Independent and honest - no pay-to-rank; rankings are not for sale.
- BINDING: Medically reviewed content with E-E-A-T, including a named medical
  reviewer (Francheska Capistrano, RMT, MPH).
- FTC affiliate disclosure is present and expected to remain.
- Voice: honest, answer-first, specific, no hype.

## Evidence on Hand

- Real provider lineup and ranking: Hone Health (#1), TMates, Male Excel,
  Taurus Meds, Dude Meds, Fridays, Brightmeds.
- Operator-supplied review figures: Hone 4.8/12,482 Trustpilot; Male Excel 4.5;
  Brightmeds 4.7. Other providers' figures are intentionally unset.
- Medical reviewer profile; deep articles sourced to PubMed/FDA/MedlinePlus and
  major reviews.
- Do NOT fabricate testimonials, customer counts, prices, or statistics beyond
  the sourced/operator-supplied figures above.

## Product Principles

1. Trust through independence: rankings reflect genuine evaluation, never
   payment, and we say so plainly.
2. Medical accuracy first (YMYL): every health claim is sourced, hedged, and
   clinician-directed; nothing is invented.
3. Help the visitor decide and act: the site's job is to move the right man to
   the right clinic, clearly and quickly.
4. Transparency: methodology, medical review, and affiliate disclosure are
   visible, not buried.
5. Built to be found: content is structured for search and AI-answer engines.
